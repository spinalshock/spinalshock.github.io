# Kosh

Kosh is a private finance app I built for my family. It runs on our own home server and is used by three people. It is not offered to the public.

It brings together what our bank text messages, mutual fund statements and credit card statements already tell us: what we spent, what we own, and what each card's bill is.

## Gmail access

Each family member can let Kosh read their Gmail, read-only. Kosh only looks for credit card statement emails from our banks, opens the statement PDF to read the amount due, the minimum due and the due date, and records those on the matching card. It never sends, changes or deletes email.
